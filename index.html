No fluff. No career-coach motivation. Just offensive techniques, defensive thinking, and code that does exactly what it says on the tin.

Follow the build at @nullphasor on Instagram — raw terminal content, tool breakdowns, and the kind of posts that make your feed feel like a threat feed.
